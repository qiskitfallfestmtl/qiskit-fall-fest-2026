Official website repository for the **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026** at Polytechnique Montréal, held on **November 25–26, 2026**.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://qiskitfallfestmtl.github.io/qiskit-fall-fest-2026/](https://qiskitfallfestmtl.github.io/qiskit-fall-fest-2026/)

## About the Event

The **Qiskit Fall Fest** is a worldwide series of quantum computing events organized by student communities with the support of **IBM Quantum**. 

Hosted at **Polytechnique Montréal** (Galerie Rolland), this two-day gathering brings together students, researchers, and industry leaders to explore variational quantum algorithms, photonic hardware, quantum machine learning, and hands-on circuit programming.

### Event Highlights

- **Day 1 (Wednesday, Nov 25) — Talks & Guided Workshops**:
  - Keynote talks on the future of quantum computing and photonic architectures.
  - Hands-on interactive workshops with **Qiskit (IBM Quantum)** and **MerLin (Quandela)**.
  - Student research pitches (3-minute blitz).
  - Ecosystem panel discussion with leaders from industry and research.
- **Day 2 (Thursday, Nov 26) — Quantum Hackathon**:
  - 8-hour collaborative quantum algorithm challenge.
  - Hands-on mentorship from academic and industry mentors.
  - Real quantum hardware execution bonus on IBM Quantum and Quandela QPUs.
  - Team presentations and evaluation by an expert jury.

## Website Architecture

This website is built as a fast, accessible, static multi-page application hosted on **GitHub Pages**:

- **Stack**: Pure HTML5, modern modular CSS (custom properties, responsive layout, dark/light themes), and vanilla JavaScript.
- **Bilingual (FR / EN)**: Dynamic client-side language toggle with instant in-place switching.
- **Accessibility**: Semantic HTML landmarks, keyboard navigable menus, visible focus indicators, and WCAG AAA contrast ratios.
- **SEO & Social Sharing**: Complete Open Graph, Twitter cards, canonical tags, and structured JSON-LD event metadata.
